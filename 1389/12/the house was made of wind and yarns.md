# the house was made of wind and yarns.

the house was made of wind and yarns. we all know that doesn't work well, but the house was there, standing lonely on top of the hill, soft and strong. the hill was covered with sand and grass. green grass made a good contrast with the world of black and white, white as shadows, black as the light of a black shooting star crossing in the white sky... oh, wait, the camera was at the negative mode...
